# Client Launch Kits

A reusable agency workflow for launching local service clients on Meta (Facebook + Instagram) and standing up their first paid + organic campaigns.

This kit is **process documentation only**. It contains no automation that touches Meta directly — no scripted logins, no signup automation, no credential handling. Every step inside Meta is performed manually by an authorized human under the client's own identity.

---

## What this kit is for

- Onboarding a new local service client (paving, roofing, landscaping, HVAC, etc.) onto Meta.
- Standing up a Facebook Page, an Instagram Business account, and a Meta Business Portfolio.
- Launching a first paid campaign focused on calls or leads.
- Publishing the first three organic posts so the Page does not look empty on day one.
- Creating consistent naming and tracking conventions so reporting works across clients.

---

## What this kit is NOT

- It is **not** a tool to log into Meta on behalf of a client.
- It is **not** a signup or account-creation automation.
- It does **not** handle passwords, 2FA codes, payment details, or identity verification.
- It does **not** bypass any Meta policy, review step, or platform check.

If a step requires a human to be logged into a real Meta account, the SOP says so explicitly and stops there.

## How to use it for a new client

1. Copy `client-intake-template.md` into a new client folder and fill it in with the client.
2. Walk through `meta-business-setup-sop.md` top to bottom. Do the manual Meta steps with the client present (screen share or in person).
3. Use `facebook-page-checklist.md` and `instagram-profile-checklist.md` to fully populate the profiles.
4. Use `ad-campaign-template.md` to draft the first campaign. Apply `naming-conventions.md`.
5. Use `organic-post-template.md` to publish the first three posts before any ad goes live.
6. Save the finished launch plan in the client's folder (see `a1-sealcoating-launch-plan.md` as the reference example).

---

## Boundaries (read before using)

- **Do not** log into Meta on behalf of a client using your own session as if it were theirs.
- **Do not** create Facebook or Instagram accounts on a client's behalf without them present and authenticating themselves.
- **Do not** store client passwords, 2FA seeds, or payment information in this repo or anywhere else.
- **Do not** automate signup, login, or identity verification.
- All ad account access must be granted to the agency through **Meta Business Manager partner/agency access**, not by sharing personal credentials.
